NEW IN THIS VERSION: SAY IT BETTER (A SPEAKING COACH WITH WORD-CHECKING)
 WHERE: English page > "Say it better: speaking coach".
 WHAT SHE DOES: pick a prompt (there are 24: about her, opinions, "explain it", stories, and 10 school-subject prompts), talk for 30 to 60 seconds, then get friendly coaching. There are NO marks.
 WHAT SHE GETS BACK (with word-checking on):
   - What the computer heard, with the FILLER WORDS highlighted (um, uh, like, you know, I mean, sort of, basically...). "I like football" is not counted; only fillers are.
   - Her pace (words a minute), her pauses, and how long she talked.
   - Whether she built her talk well: a reason (because...), an example (for example...), things in order (first, next, finally), a sum-up.
   - For subject prompts, which KEY IDEAS she covered (for example, sunlight, water and carbon dioxide for photosynthesis).
   - WORD UPGRADES from the Thesaurus vault (if she says "nice" five times: try "lovely", "delightful").
   - What she did well, up to 3 tips to say it even better, and a comparison with her last go at the same prompt.
   - 10 XP for a talk of 20 seconds or more, +5 XP for improving. Trophies: Confident Speaker (5 talks) and Power Pauser (a talk of 30 seconds or more with hardly any filler words).
   - She can LISTEN BACK to herself.

 PRIVACY: PLEASE READ. To turn her voice into words, the BROWSER sends what she says to an outside speech service: Google in Chrome and on Android, Apple in Safari, Microsoft in Edge.
 That is why you decide, and why it is OFF until you switch it on:
   - Parent Desk (your PIN) > "Say it better: word-checking" > On (check her words) or Off (recording only). It is remembered on THAT device only, so a new device always starts OFF.
   - Before every talk, her screen says in plain words where her voice goes, and that the Academy does not save it.
   - The Academy NEVER saves her voice or her words. They appear on the screen and are wiped when she leaves. The listen-back recording stays on the device and is deleted when she leaves.
   - Only NUMBERS are saved and synced (how many talks, filler words a minute, words a minute). Your report shows the number of talks, nothing she said.
   - With it OFF she can still record herself, listen back and tick a self-check list. Nothing leaves the device.

 PLEASE TEST IT ON HER DEVICE. I could not test the real Google/Apple speech recognition myself (it needs a real voice and their servers), so I tested everything else with a simulated one. Please try:
   1. Use Chrome (laptop or Android) or Safari (iPhone or iPad), with internet. Chrome is the most reliable.
   2. Parent Desk > Say it better: word-checking > On.
   3. English page > Say it better > "Explain how photosynthesis works, to a friend" > "Start talking". Allow the microphone when asked.
   4. Talk for about 30 seconds and say "um", "like" and "you know" a few times on purpose.
   5. Tap "I am finished". Check: does what the computer heard look right? Are your filler words highlighted?
 THINGS TO KNOW:
   - Chrome often leaves out "um" and "er" (it tidies them away). The results screen says so. She can listen back to check.
   - Speech recognition can mishear, especially with background noise or a quiet voice. It never blames her: it says the computer found it hard to hear and suggests a quieter room.
   - On some iPhones and iPads, recording and word-checking cannot run at the same time. If that happens it tells her and falls back to recording only.
   - If there is no internet, it says so and she can still listen back.

THE 4 FILES TO UPLOAD (this README is only for you)
STEPS
 1. Unzip this file so you can see the files (GitHub cannot open a zip).
 2. Go to github.com and open your repository, "Annaliesce-Academy-".
    You should see the file list, with index.html in it.
 3. Click  "Add file"  (near the top right of the file list), then  "Upload files".
 4. Drag all 4 files into the box (or click "choose your files").
    index.html, manifest.webmanifest and LICENSES-README.txt have the SAME NAMES as
    files already there, so GitHub replaces them automatically. share-image.png is
    new and simply gets added.
 5. Scroll down and click the green  "Commit changes"  button.
 6. Netlify notices by itself and updates the site in about a minute.
    To check: open Netlify, click your site, click "Deploys". The top one should say
    "Published".
 7. Open the site and refresh the page. If she has the site saved on her tablet or
    phone like an app, close it fully and open it again while online.
