HOW TO PUT THE NEW VERSION ON YOUR SITE (about 3 minutes)

You only need to upload the 3 files that are in this folder:
   1. index.html
   2. manifest.webmanifest
   3. LICENSES-README.txt
Nothing else needs to change. Her progress is kept.

STEPS
 1. Unzip this file so you can see the 3 files (GitHub cannot open a zip).
 2. Go to github.com and open your repository, "Annaliesce-Academy-".
    You should see the file list, with index.html in it.
 3. Click  "Add file"  (near the top right of the file list), then  "Upload files".
 4. Drag the 3 files into the box (or click "choose your files").
    Because they have the SAME NAMES as the files already there, GitHub replaces
    them. It does not ask. That is the automatic override you were asking about.
 5. Scroll down and click the green  "Commit changes"  button.
 6. Netlify notices by itself and updates the site in about a minute.
    To check: open Netlify, click your site, click "Deploys". The top one should say
    "Published".
 7. Open the site and refresh the page. If she has the site saved on her tablet or
    phone like an app, close it fully and open it again while online.

IMPORTANT
 - Upload into the MAIN page of the repository (where index.html already is),
   not inside a folder.
 - Do NOT delete anything. Do not touch the "netlify" folder.
 - If the site ever looks unchanged, hold Shift and press refresh, or open it in a
   private window.
