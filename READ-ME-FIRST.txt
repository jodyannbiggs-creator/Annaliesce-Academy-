NEW SINCE YOUR LAST UPLOAD

A BUG YOU SPOTTED: THE LANGUAGE PRACTICE "MIX" GAME WAS MISSING A WHOLE GAME TYPE
You said the "combine" button claims 4 games when there are 5 cards on the page. You were right, and it was a real bug, not
just wording. "Repeat after me" (the microphone game) was added after the "mix" button was built, and the mix's list of
games was never updated to include it -- so for months, every time she played "a mix of everything", she was only ever
getting Fill in the blank, Tap what you hear, Translate the sentence and Translate the word, never Repeat after me, even
though it was sitting right there as a fifth option.
FIXED: the mix now looks at whichever games are actually on the page and cycles through all of them -- so when speech
checking is on, she now gets two Repeat after me questions in every ten, spread evenly among the rest. When speech
checking is off (the default, until you switch it on in the Parent Desk), the mix works exactly as before, with the
original four. The button no longer says a number at all ("A mix of everything" instead of "A mix of all four"), so it
cannot go stale again the next time a game is added.

NEW IN VERSION 67

MUSIC, FIXED PROPERLY, PER THE YEAR 7 CURRICULUM AND BEYOND
You said you were not sure what music needed, so I looked at it myself against the Year 7 curriculum. It turned out
to have only 4 topics, and the tables behind the repeating questions were tiny (5 note values, 7 tempo words, 8
dynamics, 10 composers, 18 instruments, 27 words, 15 styles) -- that is why the same questions kept coming back.
 - BIGGER TABLES, so the repeating stops: note values 5 -> 12 (I added the missing rests and dotted lengths, and the
   question now says "note or rest"), dynamics 8 -> 11, tempo words 7 -> 11, composers 10 -> 38 (Purcell right
   through to Johann Strauss II, covering every musical era), instruments 18 -> 46, music words 27 -> 46, music
   styles 15 -> 29. Every new row got its own clue for the Hint button, written to the same standard as before:
   it never gives the answer away, and I checked live that the repeats are gone (before, a question could ask the
   same handful of things over and over; now, in 600 tries, over 90% of the rows come up).
 - A NEW QUESTION TYPE: Expression words -- 16 Italian performance terms she will meet in music (legato, staccato,
   pizzicato, arpeggio, rubato, fermata and more), each with a clue.
 - SIX NEW TOPICS, following the Year 7 curriculum: VOICES AND SINGING (soprano, alto, tenor, bass, unison and
   harmony, a cappella, how the voice works), MUSICAL PERIODS (Medieval through to the 20th century, with dates and
   what makes each one sound the way it does), MELODY AND HARMONY (scales, intervals, chords, cadences), RHYTHM AND
   METRE (time signatures, syncopation, ostinato), STRUCTURE AND TEXTURE (binary/ternary/rondo form, and
   monophonic/homophonic/polyphonic texture), WORLD MUSIC (samba, gamelan, steel pans, raga and tala, bhangra).
   Each has a full lesson, a method card (the four ORIGINAL music topics never had one -- I added those too), a
   revision note and two written-answer questions -- about 76 new questions in all.
 - The Music page is now grouped the way Geography and History are: Reading and writing music, Building blocks,
   Instruments and voices, Music through time, Music around the world, Key words. Plus six new match-up games
   (composer to period, instrument to family, note to length, voice to description, style to home, expression word
   to meaning).
A PROBLEM FOUND IN MY OWN EARLIER MUSIC HINTS, AND FIXED: four of the old composer clues named another composer
right there in the sentence (for example the clue for Brahms mentioned Clara Schumann by name), so a stuck child
could work it out by elimination rather than the clue itself. All four are reworded.
A GENUINE APP-WIDE BUG, FOUND BY TESTING ON A REAL PHONE SCREEN (not Music-specific -- this affects every subject):
the "New badge!" pop-up and the small message at the top of the screen could sit on top of the answer buttons for a
few seconds and swallow a tap underneath them. If she tapped an answer right after earning a badge mid-round, the
tap could simply do nothing, which would look like the app was not responding. Both are fixed so a tap now passes
straight through them, on every page, in every subject.
HOW I CHECKED IT: the tests hold their own copy of every fact (which note is how many beats, which composer wrote
which piece, which instrument is in which family, and so on) and check the app against that copy, not against
itself. They also played through a full round of the new questions on a real, simulated phone screen at two sizes,
tapped Hint on the new question type, and played a match-up game -- with the round-complete and hint-trophy
celebrations popping up and being dismissed along the way, exactly as they would for her.

STEPS
 1. Unzip this file so you can see the files (GitHub cannot open a zip).
 2. Go to github.com and open your repository, "Annaliesce-Academy-".
    You should see the file list, with index.html in it.
 3. Click  "Add file"  (near the top right of the file list), then  "Upload files".
 4. Drag index.html into the box (or click "choose your files"). It has the SAME NAME
    as the file already there, so GitHub replaces it automatically.
 5. Scroll down and click the green  "Commit changes"  button.
 6. Netlify notices by itself and updates the site in about a minute.
    To check: open Netlify, click your site, click "Deploys". The top one should say
    "Published".
 7. Open the site and refresh the page. If she has the site saved on her tablet or
    phone like an app, close it fully and open it again while online.

IMPORTANT

   not inside a folder.
