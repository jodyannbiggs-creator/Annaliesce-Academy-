NEW IN VERSION 54 (included)

LANGUAGE PRACTICE FOR SPANISH AND MANDARIN (the first part of queue item 10)
Where: open Spanish or Mandarin, then tap "Language practice" (next to Listening lab and Speaking practice).

1. HOW HARD? Easy, Medium, Hard or Extremely hard. Each language keeps its own level.
 - Easy: short sentences, three choices, the English meaning is shown.
 - Medium: longer sentences, four choices, the meaning is shown.
 - Hard: the meaning is hidden (a hint shows it for 1 XP), and when she translates she picks the Spanish or Mandarin herself.
 - Extremely hard: long sentences, extra wrong words to avoid, Mandarin with NO pinyin, and vocabulary words are typed.
   Mandarin shows pinyin under the characters at Easy, Medium and Hard.

2. SOUND. Every question has a button to hear it, and there is a switch on the page to turn speaking off. It is remembered and shared between her devices.
 - With speaking off (or on a device with no Spanish or Mandarin voice) "Tap what you hear" becomes "Tap the words": she sees the English and builds the sentence.
 - On a "fill in the blank" question the sound button plays only AFTER she has answered, because saying the sentence would give the answer away.
 - It is the device's own voice. Nothing is sent anywhere.

3. THE GAMES (ten questions each, or a mix of all four)
 - FILL IN THE BLANK: a sentence with a gap. She picks the word that fits (verb endings, tu or usted, agreement, joining words, measure words in Mandarin).
 - TAP WHAT YOU HEAR: she hears the sentence and taps the words, in order, from a bank that also holds extra wrong words.
   A wrong answer shows which words were in the wrong place, then the right sentence and what it means.
 - TRANSLATE THE SENTENCE: Spanish or Mandarin to English at Easy and Medium; English to Spanish or Mandarin at Hard and Extremely hard.
 - TRANSLATE THE WORD: the usual drill, using the 124 Spanish and 74 Mandarin words already in the Academy.

4. HOW IT COUNTS
 - Right answers earn XP like any other round. Wrong answers go into the Mistake Dojo (added in version 55, see above).
 - Every sentence belongs to a real lesson topic (Greetings, Family, Formal and informal and so on), so her answers count on the Coverage map.
 - The parent summary gets one new line: "Language practice: X of Y right".
 - 40 sentences in each language, 10 at each level.

THE LANGUAGE: the 80 sentences were written by me. The pinyin spelling and tone marks were checked against a dictionary, but no person has read the sentences yet (see the note under version 55).
NOT TESTED: the sound on a real device. I can only test with a pretend voice, so please try it on her device and tell me if anything sounds wrong.
